M3              22.663       3.117    7.270   15.57%     0.06%    0.00%

The 'reference' column is the median Delta of ordinary PartType1 on
the same grid.  The ratio is the whole result: near 1 means the
converted particles are dynamically indistinguishable from the rest
of the matter and asking which haloes hold them is the wrong
question; well above 1 means they really are locked in collapsed
structure.

Delta saturates at the cell scale, so nothing above roughly the
density of a halo filling one cell is resolved.  Cell sizes are
printed per run above; raise --ngrid to lower that floor and check
the ratio is stable before quoting it.
