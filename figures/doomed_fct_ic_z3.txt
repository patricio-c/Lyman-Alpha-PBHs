Read it like this: if the doomed gas sat at Delta of order 100-1000
it was already destined and never was forest gas.  If it sat at
Delta of a few, ordinary IGM is being removed and the threshold is
a real forest systematic.

'contrast' is the doomed median over the surviving median at the
same epoch.  Near the initial conditions that is the only column
that means anything: Delta is ~1 everywhere and the question is
whether the material that later converts starts out measurably
overdense.  Contrast > 1 says yes.

[!] for CDM the middle 80% of the doomed mass
    spans fewer than 4 bins, so these percentiles are set by the
    binning, not by the data.  Re-run over the range the gas
    actually occupies, e.g. --d-lo 0.5 --d-hi 2 --nbins 200.
